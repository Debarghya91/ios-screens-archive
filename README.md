# Payments Training Hub iOS app

SwiftUI iOS app for Payments Training Hub (paymentstraininghub.com).

- 100 chapters, 746 lessons, 564 Payment Cases, 500 interview questions
- 8-session guided learning plan, 10 simulators, 320 FAQs, 4 certificate courses
- Bundle ID: com.paymentforge.app
- Content is fetched from paymentstraininghub.com, not embedded.
- Lessons are hard-gated behind 12 in-app purchase products.
